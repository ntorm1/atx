# Equity long/short book baseline — declared 2026-09-19

Build one reproducible price/volume book in `atx-impl`, then let its measured failures determine the next engine changes. This is a proposed engineering baseline, not evidence of investment performance or live-order authorization. Optimize net out-of-sample results subject to capacity, turnover and risk constraints; no Sharpe is promised.

The provisional capital grid is **$10m / $100m / $1bn**, anchored at **$100m**. These are configurable research assumptions pending an owner capital target, not actual assets or demonstrated capacity. The supplied archive is the research input; the existing May–June diagnostic is too short for this book and has a confirmed adjustment exception.

1. **Freeze the first signal family and controls.**

   Use only two smoothed, skipped-month momentum signals, with raw price/volume determining liquidity eligibility:

   ```text
   momentum_252.dsl: ts_mean(delay(close, 21) / delay(close, 252) - 1, 5)
   momentum_126.dsl: ts_mean(delay(close, 21) / delay(close, 126) - 1, 5)
   ```

   Each `.dsl` file contains the expression alone. `delay`, `ts_mean`, division and subtraction are supported by [registry.cpp](../../atx-engine/src/alpha/registry.cpp), [parser.cpp](../../atx-engine/src/alpha/parser.cpp) and [typecheck.cpp](../../atx-engine/src/alpha/typecheck.cpp); 252 is a valid literal window. These are observation-count approximations, not exact calendar-month replicas.

   Prior-month exclusion follows the economic momentum hypothesis behind [French's prior 2–12 month factor](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_mom_factor.html). The 126-observation variant and five-observation smoothing are predeclared hypotheses, not separately discovered evidence. Volume is a liquidity input, not a bet on illiquid stocks. Avoid the existing template's `ts_mean(returns,252)-ts_mean(returns,21)` when claiming a compounded skipped-month return; it is a different signal.

   Three fixed treatments: single `momentum_252` control; equal blend of both; covariance-aware `shrinkage-mv` blend of both. An all-cash ledger is the accounting control. No GA, seed mutation, stacking, Kelly, conviction or regime selection in this first comparison. Existing [factor templates](../tests/fixtures/factor_templates.txt) are discovery examples, not a validated production recipe.

2. **Use the existing implementation interfaces honestly.**

   [run_combine](../src/stage_combine.cpp) already reads sorted loose files with `--alphas`, compiles them in a batch and supports `--method equal|rank|ic|bounded|shrinkage-mv|stack|regime-stack`. It applies the default rank/winsorization `WeightPolicy` to constituent signals. `--fit-begin` and explicit `--fit-end` index the bound panel; resolve dates to indices from the identity manifest, never guess indices or rely on the default full-history fit.

   The immediate arithmetic control is `combine --method equal`, then `optimize --position-mode --gross 1 --name-cap 0.01 --rebalance weekly`, then identified `report --report-aum 100000000 --replay-execution-delay 1 --replay-trade-bps 5 --replay-annual-borrow-bps 365 --replay-day-basis 365`. Supply exact input/output paths and `--combo` to report. These flags exist; this is an approximate shaping diagnostic, not an exactly neutral constrained book. [shape_book](../src/book_shape.hpp) explicitly permits residual net after asymmetric binding caps; measure gross/net/name caps per decision and after costs, and fail qualification on violations. Beta, sector and realized-trade budgets are also not enforced here.

   [run_optimize](../src/stage_optimize.cpp) supports daily and every-five-observation “weekly” schedules only. Calendar month-end, rank hysteresis and a realized-holdings callback are next implementation work, not existing flags. The partial `--trade-rate` position blend references prior target weights; it is not a turnover budget against marked holdings.

   The command sketch also needs an explicit evaluation-window driver before use on warmup-bearing blocks: optimize schedules from row zero, and `fit-begin` does not suppress orders or reporting. Evaluate both signals over the observation context, require their common readiness (first full 252-delay/five-day-mean value at local index 256), emit decisions only on declared evaluation dates, and anchor replay NAV at the first evaluation row. Do not trade the faster constituent during warmup or count warmup cash intervals as scored returns.

   Avoid three misleading shortcuts: default diagonal MVO covariance is fitted on the whole panel; `--conviction` uses the full constituent PnL history even with a fit boundary; `--participation-cap` uses final-date volume/adjusted price and constrains positions rather than actual trades. `--walk-forward` produces scratch telemetry, not a stitched refitted deployed book. Existing combiner fits use frictionless daily constituent streams, so final selection must use the actual scheduled net replay, not their fit Sharpe.

3. **Make long-history research feasible before allocating it.**

   The original ZIP contains 31,598,499 rows, 3,576 dates (2012-03-26–2026-06-15), and 24,959 distinct ID strings including zero; see the [input audit](../../atx-engine/reviews/2026-09-19-tbltickerhistory-input-audit.md). On this 16 GB machine with about 6 GB free, a dense 16-field raw grid alone is roughly 11.4 GB; 17 fields would be roughly 12.1 GB, before masks and copies.

   [attach_indexed_multi_segment_panel](../../atx-engine/src/alpha/segment_panel.cpp) allocates all source IDs first. [build_history_panel](../../atx-engine/src/data/history_panel.cpp) additionally materializes six corporate-action columns and twelve output fields before its late `compact_to_universe`; [write_panel](../src/serialize_panel.cpp) builds another complete payload buffer. `--compact-universe` therefore cannot prevent the initial exhaustion.

   The measured first annual block can use the existing builder: its 445 dates and 7,650 source IDs passed a conservative 3 GB preflight and peaked at about 1.03 GB working set. See the [allocation review](../../atx-engine/reviews/2026-09-19-annual-panel-memory-review.md). Defer a second builder until a declared block fails this bound. The proposed larger-block path is a two-pass identified price/volume builder: pass one streams date partitions and maintains 21-observation raw-dollar ADV/eligibility state; pass two loads only the union of selected IDs plus carried holdings, while retaining their valid historical observations. Preserve source segment hashes, IDs, dates, classification status, QA exclusions and deterministic ordering. Never choose today's constituents or keep only names surviving to the end.

   Start with top 1,000 names by trailing raw-dollar ADV, ADV at least $20m and raw close above $5, evaluated causally per decision. These are fixed research thresholds. Sector/type availability is a separate gate below. Storage compaction may use the union across a block, but that union must never grant earlier trading eligibility.

   Materialize annual evaluation blocks with at least 256 preceding observations for the frozen signals; carry a longer warmup if the selected risk estimator requires it. Keep only `close`, `raw_close`, `volume` and any verified classification field initially. At 600 rows × 5,000 retained IDs × 4 fields this numeric payload is 96 MB; measure all additional signal, model, serializer and solver allocations rather than treating that number as peak RSS.

   Preflight exact dimensions and a **3 GB peak working-memory budget**; halve evaluation block length when necessary, retaining the same warmup and policy, rather than changing the universe after seeing returns. The non-shrinkable floor is the complete warmup plus one evaluation row and all active/carried names: if that still exceeds budget, reject and redesign storage/solve execution instead of repeatedly halving or dropping holdings. Run one panel/evaluation job at a time; bounded ZIP ingestion can retain its measured worker setting. Compute risk one decision at a time and solve only active plus carried names; do not cache every dense risk model or solve a full 25,000-name augmented QP.

   Two necessary semantics accompany blocking: VM [LoadField](../../atx-engine/include/atx/engine/alpha/vm.hpp) currently masks historical observations by universe membership, which starves new top-N entrants of valid momentum history; separate feature-observation validity from decision eligibility. The current [replay kernel](../../atx-engine/include/atx/engine/book/replay.hpp) starts all-cash, so annual block outputs must not be silently stitched into a continuous fund. Add explicit cash/TRI-unit state continuation keyed by IDs, or label each independently initialized fold and charge its entry costs. Test continuous versus blocked equivalence before a continuous performance claim.

4. **Resolve source economics without inventing repairs.**

   `securityID=38946` (KLAC display ticker) has a confirmed ten-for-one split effective 2026-06-12, unchanged source factor 1, unchanged reported shares and an invalid OHLC row. The [issuer-backed exception audit](../../atx-engine/reviews/2026-09-19-klac-source-adjustment-audit.md) explains why both missing-mark rejection and factor reconciliation are required. A prefix accounting check does not repair the full series or establish performance.

   Extend the existing [preparation tool](../../atx-engine/tools/prepare_tickerhistory.py) with a versioned exception/reconciliation report: compare adjacent factor/price/return identities, then validate corporate-action conflicts against identified issuer/vendor records. Large returns are investigation triggers, not inferred splits; agreement among vendor fields is not independent proof. Preserve raw rows and factors. An unresolved held mark or economic adjustment fails the affected evaluation; no future-informed liquidation, zero return, backfill or silently deleted losing name.

   [SpiderRock's dictionary](https://docs.spiderrockconnect.com/docs/next/HistoricalData/Data%20Dictionaries/TickerHistory3/) calls `todayTicker` the latest alias and GICS reserved; its full-history T+1 delivery statement does not establish historical vintages. Use numeric security IDs, retain contemporaneous ticker as metadata, never use `todayTicker` for selection. `shares` is reported metadata and must not be inferred from a total-return factor. Do not use market cap as an alpha until shares timing is supported.

   Common-stock, exchange, domicile, sector, delisting treatment, borrow availability and historical publication times remain unresolved. Price/volume experiments may proceed with explicit unknown labels; calling the result an investable US common-stock book requires an effective-dated security master and corporate-action/return coverage. `--require-sector`/`--exclude-no-sector` are not common-stock certification. [sector_group_map](../src/sector_groups.hpp) takes the first nonmissing sector across the full history, so it cannot supply historical sector neutrality.

5. **Turn the signal into a constrained book, with units explicit.**

   Baseline mandate: cash plus equity long/short; target gross at most 1.0×NAV, dollar net zero, maximum name weight 1%; beta exposure within ±0.05 of a declared broad-market proxy; sector net within ±2% NAV when effective-dated classifications exist. An internally derived universe-average beta is only a labeled proxy. Unknown or violated constraints fail admission to the qualified book, while the simpler shaping diagnostic remains measurable without a neutrality claim.

   Treat combined ranked values as target preferences, not forecast dollars or expected returns. Add an `atx-impl` allocation boundary minimizing deviation from that target plus past-only covariance risk and an L1 trade penalty. Use actual marked holdings/NAV, with the already implemented engine [GrossNet/PositionCap/GroupCap/BetaNeutral/TurnoverBudget](../../atx-engine/include/atx/engine/risk/constraints.hpp) and QP solver; do not create a second solver. Signal residualization alone does not guarantee final portfolio neutrality after caps or costs.

   Fixed turnover objective: sum of absolute traded dollars / pretrade NAV, both directions counted, at most 0.20 per rebalance and at most 6.0 over a research year. Charge initial entry and emergency exits. Permit a cash-funded startup ramp under the gross upper bound instead of demanding immediate full gross and violating turnover. Explicitly prioritize mandatory risk/eligibility reductions over the budget, record any unavoidable breach, and fail qualification rather than weakening a gate silently. Cash debits mean post-fee exposures require a final check.

   Compare daily, weekly and true calendar month-end decisions. Add a fourth policy: weekly buy/hold hysteresis, enter long above the 80th percentile and retain until below the 60th; symmetric 20th/40th short thresholds, with risk/eligibility exits taking precedence. This is not currently exposed. [Novy-Marx and Velikov](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20721) study buy/hold spreads as cost mitigation; the proposed thresholds are frozen hypotheses, not their estimated optimum. [AQR's trading-cost research](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Trading-Costs-of-Asset-Pricing-Anomalies) supports testing slower momentum under costs rather than assuming paper returns survive implementation.

6. **Freeze research splits, trials and evaluation before running.**

   Warmup begins with the archive; training decisions: 2013-04-01–2019-12-31; validation: 2020-01-01–2022-12-31; sealed final holdout: 2023-01-01–2025-12-31. The already inspected 2026 window is engineering evidence, not a pristine holdout. Keep validation/final strategy metrics unopened until the declared data gates pass. Past observations may initialize indicators, but every fitted covariance, combiner and classification must have a cutoff preceding its decision/effectiveness boundary.

   First implementation block is explicitly **2013-04-04–2013-12-31**, revised from the assumed April 1 start before any strategy metrics: stored source-date metadata has only 253 preceding observations on April 1 and 256 on April 4. Verify the accepted-input dates after preparation; if they cannot supply the full 256 observations, fail instead of moving the start or shortening warmup implicitly. This readiness correction leaves the overall training/validation/final partition intact; see the [bounded panel design](../../atx-engine/reviews/2026-09-19-bounded-equity-panel-design.md).

   Freeze **12 strategy recipes = 3 signal/combiner treatments × 4 decision policies**. All risk/universe thresholds above stay fixed; no threshold or sign search. First run the equal-blend weekly control on a training-only block. Tune numerical solver stability on analytical fixtures, not on validation PnL. Every attempted variant, failure, rejected run and prior inspected strategy goes into an append-only trial ledger with parent IDs, exact recipe, source exclusions, fit boundaries, runtime/RSS and result/failure hashes. Data-only repairs receive a new dataset version and linked rerun, never erase old trials.

   The recipe manifest must expand every operative default before validation: constituent rank transform, 2.5% winsorization, gross 1; covariance combiner complete-case MLE/Ledoit–Wolf auto shrinkage (`CombinerConfig.shrinkage=-1`), implementation digest and fitted intensity; allocator covariance definition/lookback/shrinkage, target scaling, covariance-risk coefficient and L1 coefficient; rank ties, undefined signals and hysteresis state rules. Allocator coefficients and those new state rules are not implemented/frozen by this design note: their explicit values are a validation prerequisite, not an uncounted tuning allowance. Twelve names alone do not establish twelve trials if these values vary.

   Train the covariance combiner on training constituent returns, use validation once to select a recipe, then freeze it and refit only on data ending 2022-12-31 before opening final results once. If actual annual refitting is desired, it is a separately frozen schedule with fit cutoff before each evaluated year; current scratch `--walk-forward` is not that implementation. Never reuse the sealed final period for admission or another search after failure. [Bailey and López de Prado's DSR paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) explains why trial accounting remains necessary despite a holdout; DSR cannot cure bad data or timing.

   Frozen covariance weights require a new fit-once/apply-frozen artifact keyed by constituent DSL identities, training-statistics parents and cutoff. Current `run_combine` always refits the loaded panel; its `.weights.txt` is output-only. Do not replace the declared 2013–2019 training fit with an annual block's 256-observation warmup. Validate full versus blocked application of identical frozen weights before the 12-recipe comparison; the static equal blend needs no estimated weights.

   Score all 12 with the identified daily cash/holdings ledger: final NAV, net/gross return, observed daily Sharpe with its explicit convention, block-bootstrap uncertainty, drawdown, actual dollar turnover, annual turnover, long/short contributions, trade/borrow costs, concentration and constraint breaches. Report each validation year and the aggregate, including failures; no “best seed” reporting. Qualify only if accounting/data/risk gates pass and net validation performance is positive after base costs; select by net validation Sharpe, breaking ties toward lower turnover and participation, without claiming statistical certainty from a point estimate.

   Base scenario: 5 bps per absolute dollar traded and 365 bps/year simple short financing, actual calendar days/365. Prespecified stress pairs are (0,0), (10,1000), (25,3000) in those same units, at each capital level. These are assumptions, not observed broker rates. The initial zero-rate case is attribution only. Locates, recalls, long/debit cash financing, spreads, settlement and executable prices remain missing; they block promotion even if this stress passes.

   Use actual trades divided by 21 strictly prior observations of raw-dollar ADV; show unknown counts, distributions and maxima without clipping. Enforce a proposed 1% ADV per-name trade limit only once causal trade constraints are wired; the existing position participation box is not equivalent. A capital-scaled fixed-bps replay with identical percentage returns demonstrates no capacity. Capacity needs chronological impact/spread costs, fills or participation-limited execution, residual orders and subsequent holdings; until then report liquidity feasibility and stress assumptions, and retain `strategy_capacity=unavailable` as in [replay_diagnostics.cpp](../src/replay_diagnostics.cpp). [Cvxportfolio](https://www.cvxportfolio.com/en/stable/constraints.html) separately defines turnover and traded-volume constraints.
