# Frozen pre2023 evidence protocol

The executable `evaluation-config.json` is frozen by exact content and
independent SHA before native measurements. It preserves the original ten
directions plus ownership breadth change, split-adjusted ownership change and
operating profitability. Annual asset growth resolves to the already included
at_gr1 economic definition and is counted once: 13 primary candidates, 78
primary tests over two endpoints and three horizons, for each universe.
All exploratory fields are counted separately with frozen registry signs.

`golden_evaluation.evaluate_native` admits complete panel and physical label
generations through their fixed public validators. It streams one date's
cross section and one candidate/horizon time series at a time. It measures
conditioned qualified returns when available, and reports unsupported tests
with p=1 rather than dropping candidates or denominators. All supplied
window/feature/benchmark/cost boundaries are checked before any filtering.

The endpoints are daily signed Spearman IC with average tie ranks and signed
NYSE quintile long-short spread; minimum 100 names/date, at least 20 NYSE
reference names and 126 usable dates. Value weights use independently
qualified pre-entry USD capitalization with a 5% per-name cap. Equal-weight
spread is retained. HAC lag is max(h-1,21) with NULL positions preserving
calendar spacing; normal-approximation p-values and confidence intervals are
reported. Degenerate or insufficient tests are non-testable with p=1. BH FDR
includes each complete frozen family. Yearly mean/sign evidence, turnover,
cost sensitivities, liquidity exclusions and incremental partial-rank IC
against log_me, ret_12_1 and rvol_21d are retained.

Turnover is the L1 change in consecutive formation-date target long-short
weights including closing missing names and entry from cash. Cost sensitivity
applies the frozen 0/10/25/50bps grid to that turnover. These are formation
portfolio sensitivities, not a reconstructed live overlapping-horizon strategy,
execution estimate or net profitability claim. Missing qualified ADV is
reported explicitly. Full target/date outcomes are streamed to immutable
JSONL; report publication follows source/code/config rechecks.

No actual return measurement has been performed in this delivery. Synthetic
tests verify that conditionally qualified native rows reach the same streaming
endpoint code and that forbidden/unsupported rows cannot pass. Required
source coverage, actual 5/21/63 predictive evidence and consumer acceptance
remain open. Prior research exposure is acknowledged; no untouched holdout
claim or result-driven tuning is permitted.
