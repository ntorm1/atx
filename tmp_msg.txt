Round 6, part 1. audit-cost-model.md defect #2: the ORATS
crossing_fraction_complex = 0.53 at api/backtest/backtest.hpp was
unreachable because vol_edge_frictions hardwires SpreadKind::VolTicks,
whose half-spread carries no crossing factor. Fixed at the wiring rather
than by switching to QuoteSide (which needs per-fill recorded quotes this
book does not have) -- a constant reachable only from a path nothing
selects is how it came to be dead.

The reconciliation matters more than the flag, and it does not flatter
the strategy:

  * Christoffersen et al. (RFS 2018) 6.41% ATM relative spread is an
    EFFECTIVE measure -- the crossing discount is already inside it, so
    multiplying it by 0.53 would count the discount twice. The vega
    book therefore charges the SAME number at the new default and the
    test pins that bit-exactly.
  * The engine width (cost_half_spread_vol_pts) had no measurement
    behind it and was crossed at 1.00. It is now documented as a QUOTED
    width; 0.5 vol points was already ~1.9x too cheap as an EFFECTIVE
    charge. Applying 0.53 to 0.5 is the flattering half of the
    correction and both doc blocks say so.

Also: the vega book now reports names/day, turnover on signed per-1u-
gross weights, and the max drawdown of its own cumulative curve beside
the floor''s; --eiv-target-entry-lag rebuilds the iv-change target with
its entry leg N sessions earlier (round 5''s decisive EIV swap, made
runnable); and bench_term_slope is read from the LAGGED f4_term_slope
feature instead of the target-side row, so its lag-2 figure finally
means what it says. The panel builds f4 as exactly the same difference,
so lag 0 is unchanged.
