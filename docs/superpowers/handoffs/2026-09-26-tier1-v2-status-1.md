## 5. Which features look most promising for tradeable alpha (prior, not measured)

Full table, reasoning and citations: `2026-09-26-tier1-v2-alpha-priors.md`. **Nothing here is an atx-db result** —
the research lane has only run on fixtures; node 1.13 produces the first real numbers.

| Rank | Family (catalog ids) | Why (post-2012 / post-cost evidence) | When atx-db can grade it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Equity issuance / net payout** (`share_issuance_1y`, `net_equity_issuance`, `net_payout_yield`, `buyback_yield`) | Survives in big stocks (Fama–French 2008), low turnover, economic story on both legs; JKP replicates | After fundamentals B1 (2.7) → graded 4.3 |
| 2 | **Cash-based operating / gross profitability** (`cash_profitability`, `gross_profitability`, `operating_profitability`; planned `cop_at`) | Ball et al. 2016: cash-OP subsumes accruals, highest Sharpe of the profitability family; very low turnover | B1 → 4.3 |
| 3 | **Crash-resistant momentum** (`pct_from_high_252d`, `momentum_12_1`; planned `frog_in_pan`, `ret_12_1`) | 52-week high and continuous-information momentum are less crash-prone than UMD; survive realistic costs | **Buildable now** from retained prices → first graded at 1.13 |
| 4 | **Quarterly profitability / profit growth** (`roe_q`, `roe_q_change_yoy`, `tax_expense_change_yoy`) | HXZ ROE, ChTax (OSAP t=11.3); the filing clock costs the early drift | B1 → 4.3 |
| 5 | **Cash-flow / EV value** (`ebitda_to_ev`, `cfo_to_price`, `fcf_yield`) | Enterprise multiple works in large caps; main value is hedging momentum and quality | B1 + current ME → 4.3 |
| 6 | **Earnings-announcement return** (`ear_m1p1`) | Only earnings-news signal dated at the 8-K acceptance; drift weaker in large caps since mid-2000s | Events wave 3.9 → 4.3 |
| 7 | **Short interest / days to cover** | Lightly-shorted long leg has alpha; no borrow-fee data | Needs U2 fetch → 4.5 |

Likely to disappoint as standalone alphas: book-to-market alone, size, short-term reversal (cost-bound), illiquidity
level, Sloan accruals, SUE on the 10-Q filing clock, asset growth alone, the 52-row growth-variant zoo, standalone
IVOL/MAX/BAB, long-term reversal, dividend yield.

**Most tradeable object:** a pre-registered multi-theme composite (issuance + cash-OP + EV value + 52-week-high/12-1
momentum + ROE/ChTax), weighted by hypothesis family and frozen before any fundamental-wave return is read. Combining
~5 low-correlation themes gives roughly 1.7× a single leg's Sharpe, and it is the only object with a realistic
chance of clearing t > 3 on this sample.

**Power reality:** the graded selection sample is ~10.8 years (2013–2023), so t > 3 needs an annualized L/S Sharpe of
~0.91 (not the 0.79 R-7 quotes for the full 14.5 years); a one-sided replication pass needs ~0.50. Most true published
anomalies (post-decay SR ≈ 0.2–0.3) will end `candidate`. The 2.5-year holdout confirms sign consistency, not
significance. Biggest power levers: pass the 4.2 external-validation gate and shrink toward JKP/OSAP priors;
composites; U4 pre-2012 prices.

Out-of-plan ideas with a high prior: residual momentum (buildable now, in no wave), insider buying (module exists,
not in the DAG), options IV spread/skew via atx-vol surfaces.
