## Event and accounting contract

`prepare_execution_objective_transitions` accepts independent cash and stock event spans. `extract_execution_signal_transitions` returns a single AlphaStreams inside its cash wrapper, existing claim balances/ledger, and separately stock-indexed event dispositions and recognition records. A stock context refuses older extraction APIs, preventing silent omission of event diagnostics. Empty stock spans follow the prior cash/default numerical/hash route. Larger retained object overhead is not claimed identical; the fixture compares same-source branches, not a prepatch binary golden.

Each stock event binds predecessor/successor IDs and identity proofs, role-manifest SHA, source namespace, completion/basis proof, exact predecessor reference raw and adjusted prices, completion interval, public availability, first strictly known recognition mark, positive rational exchange ratio, successor observed raw/adjusted recognition prices, optional nonnegative fixed USD/share component and explicit policy enums. All prices/scalars remain required in the event schema even for inactive events; only active in-role events require matching successor axis/observations. The numerator and denominator are individually bounded to 1..1e9. Affirmative component exclusion prevents assuming that a merger already embedded in vendor adjusted returns can safely be applied again.

With signed predecessor marked dollars H and predecessor reference raw price P, predecessor research units are H/P. Signed successor units are (H/P)*(numerator/denominator), valued at the qualified successor raw mark; optional fixed cash is (H/P)*fixed_cash_per_share. The gross recognition bridge is delivered successor value + fixed cash claim - removed equity. Adjusted marks remain the subsequent total-return ratio convention. This is continuous research-share-equivalent accounting: it does not round to broker whole shares, invent fractional cash-in-lieu, prove actual account delivery, establish borrow locate, or forgive an inherited short obligation.

All existing successor equity is marked once before accumulated deliveries are added. This two-phase order is independent of successor/predecessor axis order and supports multiple predecessors delivering to one successor. Finite aggregate checks cover delivery, resulting holdings and all NAV components. Mandatory conversion has no modeled execution fee or turnover. Predecessor queued targets are canceled; successor targets remain frozen and net against the newly combined holding at actual entry, with ordinary cost/participation logic. No successor is promoted into decision membership because of a future event.

Only known events affect formation. Publication between a mark and decision already excludes the extinct predecessor and its still-accounted positive locked equity from fresh target capital; targets queued before publication retain their dollar values. Recognition waits for the first mark strictly after both effective upper bound and public availability. Pre-role events retire predecessors without invented opening positions or claims. Outside-axis predecessor and after-role events are hash-bound, reported and numerically inactive, without requiring an irrelevant successor axis. Potentially active event chains or terminal successors are explicitly refused in this bounded recipe; simultaneous conflicting cash/stock predecessor events and duplicate IDs are invalid.

Fixed cash components remain nonspendable signed claims with existing reserve/continued-modeled-carry policy. Stock recognition's fixed cash contributes to the common receivable/payable/carry arrays, but cash recognition/event-use indexes refer exclusively to cash events. Stock recognition and its full value bridge have separate records/arrays; the cash-only recognition PnL array does not double-count the stock bridge. Stock-only contexts still allocate cash diagnostics. Settled cash never increases from an unknown payment.

The old equity pays its modeled borrow for the conversion interval. Thereafter delivered short successor equity is charged the existing qualified successor modeled rate on actual elapsed days; a missing required rate refuses. A negative fixed cash component continues the predecessor rate and reserves cash. These are explicit research conventions, not evidence of broker loan novation or discharge. Material unmodeled negative cash remains a refusal. No automatic terminal liquidation or payment is added.
