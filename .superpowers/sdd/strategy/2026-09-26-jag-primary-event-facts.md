## Confirmed ordinary-common-share terms

Parsley Energy announced completion on January 10, 2020. Eligible Jagged Peak common shares converted at **0.447 (447/1000) Parsley Energy Class A shares per JAG share**; the issuer release was timestamped **08:51 Eastern, January 10 = 13:51 UTC**. There was no general fixed cash purchase-price leg; fractional-share cash is a separate entitlement. [Issuer release distributed by PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parsley-energy-completes-acquisition-of-jagged-peak-energy-300984916.html).

JAG's successor filing confirms completion January 10 and that JAG common shares ceased NYSE trading before that day's open. The legal surviving subsidiary, Jackal Merger Sub A LLC, is **not** the consideration security. The tradeable consideration is Parsley **Class A**, ticker **PE**. The filing does not establish the exact final tape trade or the archive security-ID mapping. [JAG successor Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1685715/000119312520005402/d864557d8k.htm).

The SEC index displays acceptance **2020-01-10 16:06:40** for accession **0001193125-20-005402**. This is a separate filing clock; this note does not equate acceptance with observed vendor delivery or infer an unstated timezone from that page. [SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1685715/000119312520005402/0001193125-20-005402-index.html).

OCC memorandum 46324 corroborates completion before the January 10 open and identifies **PE CUSIP 701877102**. Its options adjustment is 44 PE shares plus fractional cash for 0.7 share per former 100-share contract. Those are options mechanics, not an equity-account delivery receipt. The memo says the fractional cash amount was then undetermined and cash settlement delayed. Its January 13 PE1 options-symbol change must not be used as the equity event date. [OCC memorandum on MIAX](https://www.miaxglobal.com/sites/default/files/alert-files/JAG_Merger_46324.pdf).

## Fractional entitlement and delivery limits

Agreement sections 3.3(b), 3.3(h) aggregate a holder's certificates/book entries, issue whole consideration shares, and pay fractional cash without interest. The cash formula uses the fractional entitlement times Bloomberg-reported PE Class A VWAP over the five consecutive trading days immediately preceding closing. Exchange-agent/DTC procedures occur as practicable on or after closing; the agreement supplies no actual account-credit time. The exchange ratio has intervening capital-change protections; the completion filing reaffirms 0.447. [Executed merger agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1594466/000119312519267260/d820520dex21.htm).

Neither actual fractional cash, delivery time, nor broker short-loan treatment is established here. A continuous-share-equivalent research conversion must explicitly disclose that it does not reproduce holder-level whole-share rounding or its cash-in-lieu payment. It must not call unknown fractional cash zero or invent a settlement date.
