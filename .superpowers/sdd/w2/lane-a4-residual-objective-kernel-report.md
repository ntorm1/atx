## Implemented contract

New lightweight `factory/objective_ic.hpp` declares immutable shared context and worker-owned scratch. `src/factory/objective_ic.cpp` privately owns Eigen projection, ranking, label preparation and inference. Root registration is the new CPP and focused `tests/factory/factory_objective_ic_test.cpp`; no CMake/config consumer defaults were edited here.

Preparation requires the scored Panel's explicit full ordered session, decision, mark-time and security-ID axes/namespace, caller-pinned I2 axis/content hashes, and a price-source SHA matching the I2 prices parent. Shape alone and hashes read back from the object are not accepted as an external binding. Callers still assert the association of a bare Panel with their supplied source identity; no loader/feed authentication is claimed. Actual consumed price bits, presence, optional decision restriction and return guard are captured into the context identity in addition to source/recipe identities. Context retains immutable I2 ownership; caller spans need not outlive preparation.

Marks obey `mark[d] < decision[d] < mark[d+1]`; labels enter at `d+delay` with delay>=1 and end at `entry+h`, strictly before the resolved maturity cutoff. Horizons are exactly 21/63/126. Both endpoint prices must have source presence and be finite positive; a guard-count change excludes the interval. Future membership never determines regression support. The supplied Panel mask must describe observed-price presence; clock-qualified economic membership comes from I2, optionally intersected with an explicit decision restriction. The price series must have the caller's intended return/corporate-action basis; this kernel does not invent terminal prices or prove total-return quality.

I2 extraction supplies all seven continuous columns, positive PIT cap and FF49, with strict prior evidence clocks. Partial warmup becomes a per-date `ExposureUnavailable` state; identity/axis/clock/config errors remain hard errors. Missing-name policy, minimum names and original known-plus-unknown member coverage are explicit. No equal-cap, generic industry, static broadcast or missing-exposure fallback exists.

WLS minimizes `sum sqrt(cap_i)*(s_i-X_i*b)^2`. The design therefore uses normalized fourth-root-cap row multipliers. It contains an intercept, the seven continuous exposures, and FF49 contrasts omitting the lowest observed category. Columns are rescaled without changing their span. Full column rank and residual degrees of freedom are required; deficient dates are unavailable. Extreme dynamic ranges that cannot support squared row weights are refused, rather than underflowing to zero or becoming equal-weight fits.

The candidate is scaled by a positive per-date maximum before fitting. Residuals therefore preserve ranks, not original signal units. Before ranking, a numerical error threshold `64*epsilon*(columns+1)*(1+max|fit|)` distinguishes projection dust from a measurable residual. This is not an economic amplitude filter. Exact exposure proxies are reported as degenerate/unavailable; a small genuine residual is not intentionally erased. Weighted orthogonality error/effective rank/support counts are exposed per date.

Residual fitting sees only decision-time support and finite candidate values. Ordinary tied Spearman is then evaluated on finite mature signal/label pairs, with paired coverage relative to frozen fitted decision support. This evaluation pairing does not feed back into residual formation. Missing dates stay missing. HAC uses mean-centered valid IC influence and zero influence only at missing calendar entries; lags retain their original session spacing. Declared HorizonAwareV3 uses uniform lag `max(h-1, rule-of-thumb(calendar_span))`, small-sample variance `S/[n_valid*(n_valid-1)]`, guarded Bartlett fallback, and explicit effective-kernel/fallback status. Mean, SE, t and unannualized HAC IR (`t/sqrt(n_valid)`) are separate fields. Minimum valid dates and minimum calendar-date fraction gate inference; sparse means can remain descriptive without an inference claim.

Rank persistence compares consecutive valid calendar decisions on matched IDs, never compressed gaps. `1/(1-rho)` is only a diagnostic proxy, reported when rho>=0 and its distance from one exceeds 64 epsilon; it is not actual turnover or realized holding time. IC half-life fits log absolute horizon means on the same common valid dates for all three horizons, requires consistent nonzero signs and a negative fitted slope, and reports the common support and fit R². No calibrated uncertainty/alpha admission is inferred from three points.

The declared memory envelope includes retained I2 bytes, worst-case cached rows/labels and the configured number of worker scratch allocations. It conservatively charges private Eigen workspace; it is an admission model, not measured RSS. Input price/VM allocations are separately owned. Callers must honor the declared worker count; constructing arbitrary additional scratch objects is not centrally policed. There is no allocation-free hot-loop or throughput claim.
