**WAIR / Wesco Aircraft.** The issuer's [GlobeNewswire completion release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/01/09/1968543/0/en/Wesco-Aircraft-Acquired-by-Affiliate-of-Platinum-Equity-Combined-with-Pattonair-at-Closing.html)
is stamped January9 10:09ET and reports completion and cessation of NYSE trading.
The [closing8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378718/000110465920002480/tm201225d2_8k.htm)
states the January9 merger converted eligible outstanding shares to USD11.05cash;
the precise legal effective instant is the Delaware certificate filing time, not
specified there. Trading was suspended before that day's opening. The amount was
already public in the [September2019 merger proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1378718/000104746919005228/a2239644zdefm14a.htm).
Treasury/affiliate and properly appraised dissenting shares have exceptions. No
CVR or stock consideration appears in the ordinary-share conversion term. The
wire's completion timestamp must not be presented as an observed payment time.

**BOLD / Audentes.** [Astellas' issuer release on PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/astellas-completes-acquisition-of-audentes-therapeutics-300987461.html)
is stamped January15 08:22ET and states completion of the USD60-per-share cash
tender and subsequent merger. [Nasdaq ECA2020-8](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2020-8)
confirms common stock, CUSIP05070R104, last trading January14, and merger effective
before the January15 opening; formal suspension starts January16. The Nasdaq page
is an updated notice, so its original page date is not the clock for the later
completion text. Use the issuer wire for the publication clock. No stock/CVR
component is stated in the common-share consideration.

**ARQL / ArQule.** [Merck's own completion release](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-completes-acquisition-of-arqule/)
is stamped January16 08:34amEST. It states USD20cash tender consideration and the
same cash right for untendered shares after that day's merger, less withholding
and without interest. Payment was accepted and expected promptly, not confirmed
settled. [Nasdaq ECA2020-6](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2020-6)
confirms CUSIP04269E107, common stock, merger before the January16 opening, last
trading January15 and formal suspension January17. Again, do not backdate an
updated exchange notice from its original page date. No stock/CVR component is
stated.

**THOR / Synthorx.** [Sanofi's issuer release on PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sanofi-completes-acquisition-of-synthorx-inc-300992177.html)
is stamped January23 09:34ET. It confirms completion, USD68cash per share,
acceptance of tendered shares and the same cash right for untendered shares after
the merger, without interest and less withholding. The [issuer-hosted release](https://www.news.sanofi.us/2020-01-23-Sanofi-Completes-Acquisition-of-Synthorx-Inc)
and [SEC closing8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1609727/000119312520013091/d858447d8k.htm)
corroborate the transaction. The release promises prompt payment, not a shareholder
cash settlement timestamp. No CVR or stock component is stated.
