## Actual behavior

`FitnessCfg.objective_rule` defaults to `LegacyV1`. Explicit `ResidualHacIcV2` invokes the causal residual kernel on the already evaluated candidate signal, before any stream extraction/backtest. All three h21/63/126 inferences must be defined; the selection scalar is the signed, equal-weight mean of their HAC IRs. No absolute-value orientation search occurs. Horizon mean/SE/t/HAC IR/effective kernel/fallback, residual support, persistence and half-life diagnostics are returned under their own names. This is a training IC score, not out-of-sample fitness, P&L, Sharpe or DSR. The report leaves those unrelated legacy metrics NaN and produces no P&L descriptor.

`prepare_residual_fitness_binding` checks the scored Panel against the prepared context's consumed price/presence hash once. It retains the immutable context and borrows the exact Panel object; the Panel, its borrowed backing and its object identity must remain alive, unmoved and immutable while used. Per-candidate checks are pointer/shape checks, without another panel hash. Exposure/axis/source/maturity/member/return-guard identities remain inside the original prepared context. Other VM feature columns are candidate inputs, not forward-label inputs. The new numerical-recipe string explicitly names the reviewed QR complement and cancellation-aware floor.

Search reuses one residual scratch per worker across candidates/generations. Before constructing `DetPool` or allocating its threads, it requires an explicit positive worker count within both the context's declared count and context-plus-scratch byte budget; auto sizing is refused in this mode. Existing VM/search/result allocations remain outside that execution-workspace budget, as before. A direct fitness call may supply exclusive worker scratch or prepare one call-local scratch. No population-sized signal cache is added.

Residual mode requires the existing raw IC screen to be explicitly `DisabledV1` and refuses a supplied raw-screen cache. It also refuses nonempty P&L pools, checkpoint/resume, strided fidelity, weak panels, raw-output fingerprint dedup, non-scalar selection, execution/cost/turnover/capacity overlays and multiplicative DSR selection. These are checked failures, not silently disabled settings. A future residual coarse gate must use these exact residual values and support; raw IC near zero is not a valid veto for residual alpha.

Unavailable residual evidence uses `-infinity` with the distinct `ResidualUnavailable` origin. It remains in canonical/all-scored/trial accounting and has a named result record; it cannot beat a meaningful negative score, receive operator credit or enter the returned candidate list. VM evaluation failures have a separate named status. An optional codec tag preserves the unavailable origin; active residual resume is refused, and a legacy resume containing that origin is also refused. Valid and unavailable per-candidate IC records are returned once per distinct attempt. These in-memory counters/records are not a durable E2 registry completion claim.

Every Factory/library admission entry and PoolView scoring explicitly refuses residual mode. Their existing realized P&L/DSR/holdout gates cannot certify an IC-only report; no residual candidate is silently rescored under a legacy recipe. Callers can use the actual SearchDriver and direct fitness APIs now, with library admission deliberately awaiting a qualified residual-plus-delayed-net route.
