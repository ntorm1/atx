# Independent sampled residual-screen design review

2026-09-26. Reviewed the root/pool5 `SampledResidualEquivalenceV1` proposal before implementation. **Suitable for source implementation as an explicitly disabled-by-default experimental mode**, subject to the contracts below. This is a design verdict, not source/runtime approval, calibrated recall, pruning or speed evidence.

The sampled quantity must be the same decision-only WLS residual and ordinary paired tied Spearman used by the full residual objective. One fixed look uses a shared deterministic priority order, with separate quarter quotas inside each horizon's own mature window. No invalid-date replacement or candidate-dependent redraw is allowed. Priority must depend on a declared seed, stable session key and algorithm version, not signal/family/trial/sign, numeric input values or the prepared content hash. Full context identity remains independently bound for cache validity. Appending an unused future tail or changing future data must not redraw an unchanged maturity-window sample.

For stratum q, calendar size Nq and preselected count mq define w=Nq/mq. The owner's clarified estimator is `sum(w*IC_valid)/sum(w_valid)`, a ratio estimate of the full valid-date mean under candidate-independent sampling. Calendar-weighting the separately computed quarter means would instead change the target when valid fractions differ. Preserve selected and valid counts, weights, the estimated valid denominator, and missing-support reasons; unsampled or invalid dates are not observations with zero IC.

The proposed influence is `u[d]=w[d]*(IC[d]-mean)` on sampled-valid original calendar dates and zero elsewhere. A Bartlett lag sum divided by the squared weighted valid denominator, with an IID zero-lag floor, retains sampling inflation through its squared-weight diagonal. Do not add a finite-population discount that silently removes this uncertainty, or describe the sample as full-history precision. Recommended finite-sample admission uses Kish effective count `(sum w)^2/sum(w^2)` at least128, at least two valid dates in each stratum, and its corresponding small-sample correction. Require the declared lag at least2h to be supportable by the calendar; otherwise pass through rather than silently shorten it. These asymptotic estimates and a fixed pseudorandom sample do not constitute an unconditional guarantee for adaptive candidate search.

Rejection requires every horizon21/63/126 to have adequate support, all quarters represented with at least80% selected support, finite positive SE, `abs(mean)+3.5*SE < .002`, and the existing V3 quarter-mean veto. Missing, degenerate, unsupported or uncertain cases pass through. There is one equivalence decision, not repeated sampling until rejection. Persist actual seed, quota geometry, coverage, uncertainty recipe and decision reason.

The actual union of sampled QR dates should be computed before doing sampled QR work; bypass when it exceeds7/8 of the full required dates. With shared priorities, surviving candidates may reuse sampled work and fill only the remainder. Reuse must bind the identical candidate, context and sign; paired rank reuse additionally requires identical ordered instrument IDs, not merely equal counts. There should be no duplicate sampled QR work for survivors and no full rescoring of early rejects.

The75% fraction and2048 cap are feasibility choices, not demonstrated speedups. For illustration, with assumed date-IC SD .02 and independent dates, more than1225 sampled dates are needed merely for `3.5*SE < .002` at zero mean; dependence increases the requirement. A smaller window can therefore screen correctly while rejecting nothing. Do not widen the practical margin to manufacture activity. Bounded postimplementation fixtures should establish exact metric reuse, finite/missing support weighting, horizon-specific tails, fixed sample identity, union bypass and no repeated decision; practical recall and speed remain separate unqualified gates.
