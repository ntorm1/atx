**I1 — Important — atx-db/src/atx_db/alpha_panel/ea_actuals.py:192,196. Mixed currencies across different qualified metrics silently become one currency.** Candidate conflicts are checked separately per field, then each accepted field overwrites the single row currency. Two separately wrapped tables for the same quarter, one declaring USD with revenues 120/100 and another declaring CAD with net income 12/10 and EPS 0.5/0.4, produce saleq=120000000, niq=12000000, epsfiq=0.5, currency=CAD, reason=qualified. The USD sales are thereby relabeled CAD, including their seasonal comparative, and can enter the CAD SUE history. Fix by enforcing one consistent currency across every accepted current/comparative field, or retaining qualified per-field currency and checking each seasonal pair. Add a two-table regression.

**I2 — Important — atx-db/src/atx_db/alpha_panel/ea_actuals.py:249. The denominator accepts any eight older surprises, without establishing the preceding eight quarters.** Selecting the last eight non-NULL periods skips missing quarters and accepts arbitrarily old observations. A fixture containing only March-quarter releases in nine successive years qualifies the ninth SUE at 3.5889417861890838. That is an eight-year scale, not the required prior-eight-quarter surprise scale. Fix by qualifying fiscal-quarter sequence and requiring the expected eight preceding quarterly surprises with no silent gaps; fail closed when source quarter identity/continuity is unavailable. Cover missing quarters and a change in fiscal calendar.

**I3 — Important — atx-db/src/atx_db/alpha_panel/ea_actuals.py:409. Include all seasonal comparatives and relevant basis qualification in equivalence, merge compatible evidence explicitly, and NULL conflicting inputs with reasons. Test both document orders.

**I4 — Important — atx-db/src/atx_db/alpha_panel/ea_actuals.py:492. Preserve parse lineage under its own manifest identity, retain current verified feature identities, and reject incompatible dependency snapshots where required. Add a changed-producer feature-rebuild fixture.
