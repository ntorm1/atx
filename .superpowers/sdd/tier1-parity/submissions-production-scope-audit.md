## Why the apparent smaller sets do not close the scope

The archive has 985,667 main CIK members and 5,374 history members; these are member counts, not active listed issuers or filing-bearing CIKs ([status](../../../atx-db/docs/TIER1_ACTIVATION_STATUS.md)). The failed all-form attempt `04cf947d-53bb-49b7-a276-b3c74a2a52c8` left 8,504,213 attempt rows over 37,700 CIKs; the warehouse then held 10,297,910 rows over 72,810 CIKs and 668 forms. None is a complete historical listed denominator. Companyfacts has 20,390 archive CIK members, but CompanyFacts membership selects XBRL fact availability, not all former listings or all SEC filing evidence. Prices have roughly 34,251 distinct IDs in the current controller inventory, but `ticker_history_bulk.py` (`_create_symbol_map`, `_create_line_map`) can assign `TBLTICKERHISTORY-*` IDs when no authoritative SEC identity is available. The current `sec_company_tickers` snapshot cannot map every dead/historical priced issuer. The sole 2026-09-20 symbol-directory snapshot and NULL-exchange historical bar placeholders cannot prove 2012â€“2026 US common-equity listing intervals (`universe_us_listed.py`, `ticker_history_bulk.py`; see [readiness audit](activation-readiness-audit.md)). Nor may current ticker/CIK metadata be backdated to fill that gap.

The relevant filing dependency is broader than annual and quarterly reports. `fundamental_statements.py` uses Item 2.02 `8-K` rows to derive release-date context; the reported-quarter EPS source under development also needs the matching historical `8-K`/exhibit trail. `delisting_evidence.py` consumes `25`, `25-NSE`, `15-*`, and same-CIK merger forms. `filing_context_backfill.py`, `xbrl_filing_contexts.py`, quality checks, and ownership ingestion use accession-level submission metadata. `shares_outstanding.py` derives share history from CompanyFacts points, so it does not itself justify an extra SEC CIK, but its issuer linkage and filing clock still need sound identity. A scoped policy therefore must keep **all forms and every referenced history member for every eligible CIK**, across the entire source period. A two-year or current-ticker cutoff would discard historical releases, revisions, delistings, and deceased issuers and is not authorized by the user objective.

## Production choice and later measurement

Root application: retain full scope. Missing raw acceptance strings in the
verified legacy prefix are a measured clock-coverage limitation, not a demand
to reload that prefix. The program's existing filed-date-plus46-hour daily
policy permits those rows conservatively. Do not invent exact offsets or add
a new production blocker/metadata recovery solely for legacy NULL strings.

Resume the existing full archive command from [production sequence](production-resume-sequence-2026-09-21.md) after the sole companyfacts writer is terminal and inspected: `--only submissions_load`, batch 50, the failed dataset UUID above, unchanged archive, no download stage, 1 GB DuckDB/one thread under the 2 GiB process guard. This preserves all historical forms and source evidence for later identity and eligibility decisions. Its cost is parsing the entire SEC directory and filing-bearing members; the verified prefix adds hashing, ordered row comparison, and source parsing before new writes. No wall-time advantage or future success is asserted here.

After that writer releases the database, measure: (1) submission rows, filing-bearing CIKs, forms, history references and missing members by archived CIK, including new versus verified-prefix counts; (2) distinct historical price IDs with a dated, authoritative CIK link versus vendor-only/unresolved IDs; (3) CIK anti-joins among priced IDs, CompanyFacts, current master, historical listing evidence, and submissions, broken out by trade year and dead/active status; (4) actual `8-K` Item 2.02 and delisting-form coverage for the historically listed cohort, plus accession joins to CompanyFacts and reported EPS; (5) guarded verifier/load time and peak memory by progress interval. Only an authoritative historical listed-issuer/CIK closure with explicit unresolved counts, full-history receipts, and honest coverage thresholds could justify a future scoped ingestion. The current run remains the safest path to a reviewable full-universe production measurement.
