## D1 causal contract and safe implementation

Implement new pure parsing/linking modules first, then fixture and integration checks. Proposed owned files are `atx-db/src/atx_db/security_link.py`, `sec_identity_sources.py`, `security_link_artifact.py`, corresponding new tests and a bounded artifact exporter script. Keep CLI/migration registry/shared loaders untouched. Emit a **versioned artifact table** rather than changing the active DB. Any eventual migration belongs in a newly allocated `bodies_NNNN.py` after the other owner freezes its registry; 0326/0327 are taken and 0328 is not reserved by this brief. An unregistered DDL draft may live with the lane artifact until integration. Reuse the existing historical evidence schema via an explicitly frozen interface, rather than create a competing meaning for “verified”.

Each record carries namespaced vendor ID and stable canonical **security-line** ID, CIK issuer relation, original/normalized symbol, share class/type and evidence IDs; economic `[valid_from,valid_to)`; `source_published_at`, `observed_at`, `available_at`, source revision/hash/locator; evidence and availability status; rejected/conflict reason. Canonical engine axis remains the positive vendor security ID where that is the established line key. Do not collapse multiple share classes to CIK or rekey past bars using a current company name. Separate accounting-owner IDs map onto that axis only through an eligible interval.

At decision time `t`, a link is usable only when its economic window covers the decision session and every required proof was available by `t`. Acceptance metadata must be normalized with declared SEC timezone/session-cutoff handling and raw value preserved. `available_at` is no earlier than the latest required publication/acceptance timestamp, with any declared processing lag. A filed date with an invented hour is modeled, not verified. A quarter archive's current download time is provenance, not proof that every inferred relationship was historically available.

**Observed acceptance is distinct from revision evidence and public availability.** Preserve the original acceptance stamp, the source's correction/revision metadata, and retrieval/vintage lineage separately. The SEC documents that `FILED AS OF DATE` may be post-acceptance corrected and `DATE AS OF CHANGE` identifies a post-acceptance change; quarterly indexes incorporate later corrections. Therefore an old accession and acceptance time cannot backdate a later-corrected CIK, symbol, name or other identity field. The FSDS `prevrpt` flag describes subsequent amendment: do not use its current value to retrospectively discard or revise an earlier decision. If the historical version of a required field cannot be established, retain the candidate with unresolved revision/vintage status. [SEC timestamp definitions](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions), [post-acceptance corrections](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data), [FSDS schema §5.1](https://www.sec.gov/files/financial-statement-data-sets.pdf).

An authentic acceptance stamp can verify the **acceptance event** without verifying the exact first-publication clock. The SEC supplies no first-publication timestamp and does not guarantee dissemination latency. Declare timezone/DST conversion and session/dissemination policy; a chosen fixed delay or next-session rule remains modeled availability unless independent publication evidence supports verification. Do not inherit a `verified` availability label merely because another module applies a fixed clock floor. Preserve separate evidence and availability statuses in D1 outputs. [SEC dissemination and timestamp FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions).

The plan's “filings on both sides” is a **retrospective consistency check**, not permission to leak the right-hand filing backwards. If confirmation at `t2` is required to establish a link for an earlier interval, its causal availability is at least `t2`; earlier decisions remain unresolved unless independent evidence already sufficed. Later contradictions/revisions must not overwrite the previously available state. Prefix/future-filing mutation must leave earlier bridge decisions, exports and membership bytes unchanged. Also avoid building intervals from future last-trade dates or making a later rival remove an earlier accepted candidate retrospectively. Verified identity coverage may consequently miss plan thresholds; report that honestly rather than admit reconstructed links.

Manual override CSV uses the same windows, clocks, proof hashes, reviewer/reason and deterministic priority. An override adjudicated today cannot masquerade as historically available without original dated evidence. Reject ambiguous overlap; retain all rejected candidates. Naming normalization is not evidence of equivalence.

**Required consumer implementation scope (authorized by root):** minimal adjacent exporter wiring beyond `load_id_bridge` must carry interval records and filter/partition each snapshot by the link's validity and availability. The current `dict[str,str]` plus unconditional copy loop cannot represent sequential issuers. Coordinate ownership with W2-D3, which owns acceptance-clock/fundamental consumers, for the required `points.csv`/C++ interval semantics. A forward-filled old issuer must expire when its identity interval ends; delaying a row's start alone is insufficient. Keep D3 fiscal arithmetic/clock repair and D4 classification implementation separate. D1 may publish the bridge first, but must not claim consumer completion while the static export/forward-fill remains. This is ordinary correctness work already authorized; there is no user-permission dependency.
