## Numerical contract before optimization

**AuditExact is the corrected W0 oracle contract**, including `FlatGuard::RelativeV2` and windowed sum/mean. `KernelPolicy::legacy_v1()` separately preserves pre-W0 rules/digests; it is not synonymous with AuditExact. Preserve both existing routes. Changing loop interleaving across independent instruments is allowed; reassociating a single instrument's sum, enabling FMA, or replacing its window arithmetic with a recurrence is not bit-exact. Keep new arithmetic in ResearchFast unless exact equivalence is demonstrated. Do not weaken the oracle or a legacy fixture to obtain a speedup.

Decay is a **finite** window, not recursive infinite-history EWM:

`S[t] = f*S[t-1] + x[t] - f^d*x[t-d]`, `W=(1-f^d)/(1-f)`, output `S/W` only after `d` observations and the required clean-window gate. Initialize from the same first date, omit the leave term before `t>=d`, and precompute coefficients once. At `f=1`, use `W=d` and the declared sum update. Near one, use a stable closed form (e.g. `expm1(d*log(f))/expm1(log(f))`) without recomputing transcendental functions per cell. Recenter/rebuild causally if necessary for bounded drift/overflow recovery and disclose amortized, versus strict worst-case, O(1).

**Do not assume `0<f<1`.** `src/alpha/typecheck.cpp` accepts positive factors; `alpha_widened_conformance_test.cpp` explicitly exercises **1.5 and 2.0**. For `f>1`, forward recurrence amplifies error and powers can overflow. Preserve supported semantics with a proved stable scaled implementation or explicit batch fallback, and disclose the fallback region/counters. Factor near zero, near/at one, large factor, underflowed weights, huge finite inputs and `d=1` need separate checks. Benchmark factors must be preregistered, not selected after timing. Do not silently clamp factors or change the DSL.

NaN handling is operation-specific: sum/mean and co-moment/time-regression count nonfinite values as missing; order statistics/extrema treat infinities as values; linear weighted sums can emit signed infinity. Exponential batch arithmetic also includes `0*inf` when a weight underflows, so a generic “one positive infinity means +inf” rule is insufficient. Keep nonfinite values out of finite recurrence state, maintain running counters, and use an exceptional-window fallback where needed to reproduce existing classifications and recover after the last bad value exits. Delay copies its selected observation regardless of intervening gaps; delta subtracts exactly current minus delayed. Backfill/count-nans retain their special policies.

Remove O(d) **validity pre-scans**, not the intentional AuditExact numerical recompute. Supply an internal checked-window kernel entry to VM paths that maintain exact rolling counters; retain validation in standalone scalar entry points. Warmup, no-NaN and all-finite gates are distinct. Pair validity is per pair: either nonfinite input invalidates that observation. Preserve sample covariance (`d-1`), regression first operand dependent/second predictor, corr bounds, and time-regression residual convention.

W0's relative flat test is `sqrt(ss/d) <= 1e-10*abs(mean)` with documented NaN/zero outputs. Existing sliding cancellation/reseed guards are different and tiny-window `direct_pair` lacks that exact policy parameter. Reconcile ResearchFast classification at the flat threshold when wiring it; do not reintroduce false fundamental signals from constant/near-constant windows. Retain shifted sums, tiny-window fallback and level-jump/drift repair from `ts_sliding.hpp` rather than replace them with raw second moments.

## SIMD, threads and causality

Use xsimd across independent instruments, a structure-of-arrays state layout, unaligned loads/stores unless alignment is proved, masked exceptional lanes and scalar tail for partial tiles. The core SIMD examples (`atx-core/include/atx/core/simd.hpp`) explicitly use FMA in some helpers: do not reuse a reduction/FMA helper where it changes required rounding. SIMD width/tile boundaries must not alter a lane's update or reseed schedule. Avoid SIMD division/sqrt approximations that invalidate 1e-9 conformance. Streaming needs identical arithmetic, including contraction and exceptional branches, to meet its existing bit-equality promise within each mode.

Partition **instrument tiles**, keeping each instrument's full date sequence on one task; avoid a barrier for every date. Pre-size worker scratch outside parallel bodies. `Engine::execute_range` accepts arbitrary `[j0,j1)` and strategy B's `global_dag_eval.hpp` already calls it from its own pool with non-tile-aligned cuts. Respect exact ranges and reset invocation state; no out-of-range vector store, shared-lane state or hidden second evaluation. `set_ts_pool` currently affects only the batch column path; fast tile paths need deliberate dispatch too. Never call `parallel_for` recursively on the same `DetPool`, and do not add nested eight-thread pools to strategy B workers. Full-range and chunked paths must produce identical bits in the same mode.

For delay, guard `d>=dates` and checked cell/byte arithmetic before copying. A whole-block memmove is valid only for a full-width slice; subranges must not overwrite neighboring workers' columns. Define/reject unsupported external slot overlap or handle copy direction correctly—row-by-row forward copies are not equivalent to memmove for arbitrary aliasing.

Coordinate with **W1-X1** (`tests/common/causality_harness.hpp`, `atx-impl/tests/causality_*_test.cpp`); that new harness was absent from the integration ref inspected. A1 supplies the real compiled-VM/streaming adapter, X1 owns shared protocol/registration. Perturb future prices, adjustment inputs, missingness, membership and source clocks; assert raw-byte-identical outputs through cut `t`, separately for AuditExact, ResearchFast and legacy batch policy. Include full-versus-prefix panels, cuts around warmup/reseed/gap recovery, scalar tails, arbitrary chunk partitions and worker counts. Keep a planted future-reading control to show adapter sensitivity. Full-column rank compression may inspect future ordering metadata but must never alter earlier integer counts/selected values; prove this under future extremes, duplicates, NaNs and signed zero. Do not redesign LoadField's existing membership-mask history semantics here (A-12 belongs to W4-A6); preserve them and test future mask invariance.

Fivefold eight-thread scaling is not guaranteed for bandwidth-bound kernels; report per-op and battery curves, not a selected favorable row. The <=2 ns mean target applies to ResearchFast; corrected AuditExact's O(d) chronological recompute cannot be replaced by an online sum to meet it. Faster kernels may reduce strategy B's relative CSE benefit, so the 3x cold end-to-end target remains an independent gate. Missing a target is an unresolved gate, not justification to change math or benchmark scope.
