# Goal amendment: platform and alpha in parallel

Owner direction, 2026-10-17: "Expand this goal into two parralell tracks. 1. Build a platform and infrastrucutre to facilitate the generation of Alpha 2. Use the platform to find actual alpha".

The goal now has two concurrent tracks. P9 remains the current registered execution milestone; its frozen producers, experiment order, accounting, read restrictions and acceptance rules remain binding. This amendment changes work organization and reporting, not a measured experiment's criteria. New research beyond the P9 registration requires a prospective next-milestone specification, not a retrospective extension of a rejected cell.

| Track | Outcome | Evidence of progress | Current work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Platform and infrastructure | A reliable, reusable and efficient system for generating, testing and retaining alpha research. Centralize reusable computation in atx-engine/atx-impl C++; advance SQLite integration with explicit authority and reproducibility rules. | Working capabilities with exact implementation/review/validation evidence; runtime and memory observations; reproducible continuation; explicit remaining defects. Counts of files or features alone do not establish research value. | Independently audit the platform delivered by P9, distinguish landed/partial/deferred items, and prepare the next bounded implementation priorities while the research track completes. |
| 2. Alpha research | Use the platform to discover and evaluate incremental predictive or portfolio value after modeled trading costs, financing and risk, with trial accounting and honest uncertainty. | Preregistered comparisons, accepted and rejected hypotheses, cumulative evidence and robustness checks within authorized data. Leverage-driven return increases are reported separately from alpha. A positive TRAIN result alone does not establish out-of-sample alpha. | Finish the registered cells, adoption print, permitted OD-3 disposition and research verdict; retain failures and limits. H is the accepted incremental portfolio candidate; X is the selected leverage candidate after V rejection. Final adoption statistics remain pending. |

Both tracks share provenance, data contracts and an append-only experiment ledger. The platform track receives concrete bottlenecks from research; the research track uses reviewed and pinned capabilities. Neither track may silently change the other's frozen dependencies or acceptance rules. Infrastructure success and alpha evidence receive separate verdicts. A completed research program may report that credible alpha was not established; it must never manufacture success from infrastructure delivery or additional leverage.

Parallelism means independent useful work proceeds concurrently. One code/run root operates in pool-2, one bounded data run per pool and one C++ build among our pools remain the limits. During EXE-FREEZE, the parallel platform agent is restricted to source/evidence review and implementation briefs; it does not rebuild or mutate frozen code. Post-P9 implementation must first establish the next milestone's scope and producer pins.

Resource direction also remains binding: do not wait for RAM. Reuse completed phases, stream large files and use bounded/incremental algorithms; omit optional admission-wait and host-budget arguments on the released P9 runs. A necessary limit increase must be explicit and prospective. Preserve actual resource or permission stops; do not poll RAM, retry a refused command or affect other sessions.

Completion of the P9 milestone requires separate platform and research reports, all registered cell dispositions, adoption, OD-3 read or its authorized not-read default, final accounting, freeze verdict and the complete ruling index. Platform gaps and unproven alpha remain visible in the next-milestone handover. The broader two-track direction persists after that milestone.
