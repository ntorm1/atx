**Formulas as implemented**
- WLS: session-t simple returns (raw-guarded) are regressed on X_{t-1} = [market, FF49 slots (industries under 10 names go to residual slot 49), 11 styles], weights sqrt(cap_{t-1}), subject to sum_k cap_k f_k = 0 over active industries. Solved as KKT with FullPivLU; a singular session is left unfitted. Styles: size ln cap, beta (252 d, min 126 pairs), residual vol, momentum 252-21, value be/cap, E/P ni_ttm/cap, gp_ttm/at, at/at_lag4-1, lt/at, ln ADV63$, SI = mean(z si/shares_out, z si_dtc). Each style is standardized with cap-weighted mean 0 and equal-weighted SD 1 (winsorized at 5 SD, clipped at 3; missing -> 0). Cap = me_company, else shares_out x raw. All 11 styles exist in fields-v7; the manifest lists any that are unavailable. Not done: style orthogonalization and the eigenfactor adjustment (engine eigen_adjust not wired).
- Factor cov: variance = EWMA HL 84 x (NW-5 HAC / base at NW HL 252) and correlation = EWMA HL 504 with NW 2. This is a recursive twin of engine ewma_variance_v2 / ewma_factor_covariance_v2; the tests assert agreement to 1e-10. A factor needs 63 observations; a non-PD matrix gets an eigen floor. VRA: lambda^2 = EWMA-42 of the cross-sectional mean z^2 on prior forecasts (factors equal-weighted, specific cap-weighted), active after 21 dates; cov and specific are multiplied by lambda^2.
- Specific: TS = EWMA-84 + NW-5 residual variance, blended with a structural ln-vol WLS on exposures (exponentiation-corrected) with gamma = min(1, h/252). The blend is shrunk to the cap-weighted size-decile mean with q = .1 (engine specific_risk_v2 formula). A name is forecast once it has 252 residuals.
- Bias: b = SD(z) with z = r/sigma_hat(t-1); rolling 63/252 windows; band 1 +- sqrt(2/T); kurtosis band 1 +- 1.96 sqrt((k-1)/(4T)) (USE4 App. A). Families: 64 random dollar-neutral portfolios (seed 7), the 62 factor-mimicking returns, and the book (--book-weights, which reads L3 holdings.csv as is: session_ns, instrument_id, held_weight). Outputs bias.csv and bias_summary.json.
- KO: cost/$ = (s/.02)(W/W*)^(-1/3)[2.08 + 12.08 sqrt(x (W/W*)^(2/3)/.01)] bps, with W = s x ADV$ and W* = .02 x 40 x 1e6 (Kyle-Obizhaeva 2016). The benchmark at 1% ADV costs 14.16 bps. S2-KO = S2 book with this law plus S2's 1 bps commission.
- FIM: (a + b y + c sqrt y) bps with y in % of ADV; b -.53 and c 11.21 from FIM 2018 Table VII col 10; a = 3.87 so the 1% cost equals the Table XI median of 14.55 bps. No separate commission (FIM costs are all-in). Both stress books keep S2's financing and 1% cap; S2 stays primary.
- Capacity: the book is replayed at NAV x m for m in {.5,1,2,4,8} as S2 with impact_y x m^delta; dollar outputs are divided by m (an exact scale identity, and the cap still binds). The x1 row equals S2 bit-for-bit. capacity_curve.csv reports cost/$ and net SR per multiple.
- aim-partial-v6: c_i = S2 marginal cost/$ = (hs + comm) + (1 + delta) Y sigma_i (q/ADV_i)^delta at q = theta L NAV/N; c_bar = cross-sectional median. target_i = aim_i/(1 + kappa c_i/c_bar), then each side is rescaled to its entry gross (an unpriced name gets target 0). band_i = (b/N)(c_i/c_bar)^(1/3). theta_t = theta clip(sqrt(c_ref/c_bar), lo, hi), where c_ref = mean c_bar over the book's last <= 252 decisions including today. With kappa 0 + clip [1,1] + b = dust, the result is v5 bit-for-bit. TC per date = corr(alpha/sigma^2, w) = corr(desired/sigma, w).
- Defaults (literature, not TRAIN): USE4S 84/504/NW 5-2/VRA 42 (Menchero-Orr-Wang 2011); q .1; kappa 1 and b = --dust-multiple (.1) (Garleanu-Pedersen R2.2); band exponent 1/3 (Janecek-Shreve); clip [.5,1.5] (R2.3); 252 reference decisions. Prereg C1-C3 map to --cost-shrink-kappa .5|1 --rate-clip .5,1.5|1,1.
