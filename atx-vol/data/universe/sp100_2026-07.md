# SP100 Universe — Curated Static Snapshot (2026-07-26)

## Overview

This universe file captures the S&P 100 membership as of 2026-07-26, curated as a static snapshot for surface-database backfill. It includes the SPY index leg plus 101 constituent tickers.

## Composition

- **SPY**: Index ETF leg (weight 100.0)
- **101 S&P 100 constituents**: Dual-class treated (GOOG/GOOGL both included; OSI notation preserved for symbol mapping)

## Data Characteristics

- **Survivorship bias**: By design. The snapshot captures 2026-present membership and is applied to backfill 2022 forward-dated data. This means early years (2022-early 2026) will show legitimate coverage gaps for symbols that joined after the snapshot date.
- **Symbols with post-2022 listings**: Deliberately excluded from backfill scope. Examples include GEV-style spinoffs. However, PLTR and UBER have legitimate pre-2022 option data and are included.
- **Refinement path**: Point-in-time membership file (not yet implemented). Future work can map historical membership snapshots to each backfill date for survivor-free analysis.

## Source and Dates

- **Curated**: 2026-07-26
- **Applied to backfill**: 2022 onwards
- **Source code**: `atx-vol/data/universe/sp100_2026-07.csv` (tab-delimited, effective_date | symbol | raw_weight | source | as_of)

## Known Limitations

- Early-year gaps for symbols that joined S&P 100 between 2022 and 2026
- No point-in-time membership routing (uses current members for all historical dates)
