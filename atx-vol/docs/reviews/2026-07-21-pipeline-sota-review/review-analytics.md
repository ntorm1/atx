## 1. CORRECTNESS — ranked defects

**P1-1. Dispersion vega-sizing units differ 100× between the two dispersion routes.**
`src/dispersion.cpp:490-495` sizes `straddle_qty = target_vega / (straddle_vega · multiplier)` where `straddle_vega` is per-**unit**-vol (`AmericanGreeks::vega`, dP/dσ), while the listed route `src/listed_dispersion_schedule.cpp:140,169` sizes in per-**vol-point** vega (`vega_per_unit_vol · multiplier · 0.01`). Same conceptual knob (`target_vega`), two conventions. Failure: a user running `build_dispersion_book` (projected route) and the listed schedule with the same `target_vega = 10000` gets books 100× different in size; cross-route PnL/parity comparisons (the exact comparison `research/2026-07-19-dispersion-two-route-comparison-status.md` is doing) are meaningless unless the caller knows to rescale. Session memory (obs 29167/29185) confirms this is live, not hypothetical. Fix: one documented unit on `DispersionConfig::target_vega` + conversion at the seam.

**P1-2. `derivatives.cpp` variance strip silently truncates the wings for high-vol/long-T names.**
`src/derivatives.cpp:39-51` fixes the log-strike span by quality tier (Standard ±1.5), never by σ√T. `StripTruncatedLeft/Right` flags (`:330-345`) fire only when the surface IV at the boundary is non-finite — but a parametric eSSVI/SVI surface returns finite IV everywhere, so a σ=60%, T=1y name (6σ√T = 3.6) integrates only ±1.5 and reports K_var biased low **with no flag**. Contrast `analytics_density.cpp:85-89`, which adaptively widens to `width_sigmas·σ_atm·√T` (default 6σ). Failure: `deriv_price`/`var_swap_fair_strike` on meme/biotech names or LEAPs under-marks the var leg by multiple variance points; the vol-swap `convexity_adjustment` (which uses this strip) inherits the bias. Also note two independent MFIV implementations exist (derivatives.cpp vs analytics_density.cpp) with different grids, forward interpolation (linear-F at `derivatives.cpp:78` vs log-F at `projection.cpp:285`), and truncation policy — drift risk.

**P1-3. Event-vol identifiability: the production eMove is the naive two-pillar solve.**
`analytics_aggregate.cpp:39-60` (`earnings_implied_move`) and the session's solved `implied_emove` feed `EventContext` from the **first** bracketing pillar pair via `implied_emove` (`event_vol.cpp:75-106`), which assumes one flat censored instantaneous variance across the bracket. Steep term structure between the two pillars aliases directly into eMove² (research doc §2 pitfall "differencing amplifies noise; fit σ_C from MANY expiries"). The convention sweep documents the concrete failure: AAPL fitted eMove 0.0567 vs truth 0.0208 (+173%) when no near expiry spans the event. The better joint fit (`fit_earnings_term`, non-trivially engineered and well-guarded) exists but is wired only into the earnings-repro pipeline, **not** into `VolaSession`'s eMove solve or the analytics `EventContext` path. Equal-eMove-per-event (front print richer — SOTA item B5) and non-earnings date-certain events (B7) are unmodeled. Severity P1 because `atmf_vol_ex_earnings`, `event_var_share`, and event-aware serving all consume this single scalar.

**P2-4. Mixed time-convention seam in the earnings repro default config.**
With the locked default `EarningsReproConfig::time = VolTime` (`earnings_repro_config.hpp:134`): (a) `run_earnings_repro` (`earnings_repro.cpp:165-191`) brackets/interpolates VolTime query tenors `Tq` against **Calendar365** pillar `T`s and divides a Calendar365 total variance by a VolTime `Tq` (`interior_censored_vol:66`); (b) `out.n_earn[i] = count_events_at(...)` inverts the VolTime year-fraction through the **Calendar365** inverse `ns_from_year_fraction` (`event_vol.cpp:46-49`) — a day-scale instant error that can flip an event count near a boundary, and actively censors with the wrong `nq` in the `censor_space=false` branch. The sweep doc §"Caveat carried forward to M5" honestly documents (b) and shows `nEcMatch=yes` held on the cohort, and the empirical RMSE win (0.030→0.012) is real — but it is validated-by-outcome, not by construction. Any new name/snapshot where an earnings print sits within the ~few-day convention gap will miscount silently.

**P2-5. Projection spine returns NaN for Wing/C8/CStar surfaces.**
`projection.cpp:44-83` (`slice_w`/`slice_T`) handle only Essvi/Svi; `Wing/C8/CStar16M` → NaN. Consequently `surface_insert_vol_slice`/`w_on_inserted_slice`/ShapeBlend/**event-aware serving** (`surface_eval_ex` with non-null `events`) all fail with "non-positive total variance" for exactly the curve families the fit policy routes event boards to (C8/CStar exist *because* of event W-smiles). Event-aware projection and event boards are mutually exclusive today.

**P2-6. Three delta conventions across the analytics stack.**
Analytics wings/RR/BF use **American |delta|** (`analytics.hpp:37-39` via `resolve_strike_by_delta`, strategy.hpp:207); `projection.cpp:31-40` solves **European B76 forward delta**; `contract_projection.cpp:132-270` solves American delta seeded from a carry-discounted spot-delta inversion. A "25Δ RR" from `compute_surface_analytics` is not the same strike as a 25Δ from `surface_solve_k_for_delta`, and neither matches the vendor-standard Black forward/spot delta on high-carry or deep-ITM-early-exercise names. Internally documented but a real cross-module and cross-vendor comparability defect.

**P2-7. BL density clamp-and-renormalize hides tail truncation from quantiles.**
`analytics_density.cpp:258-298`: negative BL densities are clamped to 0, endpoints copied, then pdf **and** CDF are renormalized to unit mass. `mass_before_norm` records the raw mass, but the quantiles/`prob_below_forward` are computed off the renormalized CDF, so a caller with `width_sigmas=0` + fixed ±0.6 span (or a fat-tailed long-dated tenor) gets 5%/95% quantiles silently biased inward; the truncated tail mass is redistributed pro-rata rather than placed in the tails. Default config (6σ adaptive) is safe; the knobs allow the unsafe regime with only a soft flag.

**P2-8. Carr–Lee vol swap is the zeroth-order ATM proxy only.**
`derivatives.cpp:374-412`: `K_vol = √(2π/T)·C_ATMF/(F·df)` — the ATM-straddle approximation, not the Carr–Lee strip synthesis. On a skewed equity surface this is biased relative to the true zero-correlation value and ignores the correlation correction entirely; the reported `convexity_adjustment = √K_var − K_vol` therefore mixes a skew-aware var strike with a skew-blind vol strike. Documented as first-cut; mid-life vol-swap MtM correctly refuses (NotImplemented).

**P2-9. `Calendar365` is actually the Julian 365.25 year** (`vol_time.hpp:187-203`). Deliberate, extensively documented (repin cost); consequence: all T's ~0.07% short, IVs ~3bp below an ACT/365 vendor. Not a bug, but it must be listed as a standing external-comparability bias, and the constant is mirrored (not aliased) in `portfolio_pricer.hpp`/curve.cpp — a desync risk the code itself flags.

**P3-10.** `implied_correlation_clean` (`analytics_primitives.cpp:195-215`) accepts negative `var[i]` → `sqrt(NaN)` → returns `Ok(NaN)` instead of an error; `dirty` similarly unvalidated. **P3-11.** `compute_surface_diff` hardcodes 25Δ for `d_vol_fixed_delta`/RR/BF (`analytics_aggregate.cpp:332-338`) ignoring `cfg.delta_points`; fixed-delta change uses the put side only. **P3-12.** `contract_projection.cpp:454-458` re-checks the solved delta against the terminal full-Greeks delta at tolerance 1e-7 — a different evaluation route (analytic vs configured) can differ by more than that, producing spurious `Unavailable` on a converged solve. **P3-13.** `contract_projection` `CalendarDays`/`YearFraction` expiries land at "valuation time-of-day + N days", not the 16:00 ET settlement instant that `settlement_instant_ns` (vol_time) provides — the ~0.8-trading-day front-T convention fixed on the OPRA path (obs 29205) persists in the projection templates. **P3-14.** `trading_hours_between` silently truncates spans >~20y (`vol_time.cpp:197-199`), misclassifying the excess as non-trading time — harmless in practice, unflagged. **P3-15.** `occ_ess.cpp:65` hardcodes century "20" for the 2-digit year.

Positives worth recording: the BKM implementation is correct forward-referenced (weights and the E[e^R]=1 drift both verified against the standard formulas; tests pin closed-form lognormal moments/quantiles to tight tolerances); Simpson node placement puts F on a panel boundary so the OTM kink is handled; `event_aware_w`'s censor-then-interp matches SR FLEX exactly; `fit_earnings_term`'s floor-clamp/flatness classification logic is unusually carefully reasoned; vol_time's DST/holiday math is exact-by-construction and well-tested; the sweep doc is a model of honest gate reporting.

## 2. PERFORMANCE

- **Per-query cost:** `compute_surface_analytics` at defaults ≈ 10 tenors × (2 deltas × 2 sides American-delta root-finds — the dominant cost, each root-find ~5–15 American delta evals — + ~7 scalar `iv` reads) + var-swap strip per non-RND tenor (201-node grid: one fused `evaluate_batch` IV + 201 B76 prices + 3 Simpson passes) + 2 RND grids. Good micro-hygiene: F/atm resolved once per tenor (`analytics_aggregate.cpp:112-114`), RND var-swap/BKM copy-back avoids duplicate grids (`:180-194`), density TU shares one `build_price_grid` per call, `compute_surface_diff` resolves 4 wings not 10 (`:329-338`). A dedicated `bench/analytics_bench.cpp` exists.
- **Real hot-path waste:** `curve_forward_T` (`projection.cpp:206-230`) allocates a `rows` vector, re-validates and **re-sorts** the forward pillars on every call; `surface_eval_grid` is a serial per-row loop over `surface_eval_ex`, each row paying that alloc+sort plus a fresh coordinate conversion — no shared-T amortization, no parallelism. For grid/tearsheet-style sweeps this is the first thing a profiler will find.
- **Batch availability:** projection has `iv_on_inserted_slice_batch` and `surface_eval_grid` (serial); analytics has **no** batch/multi-underlier entry (cross-sectional runs are caller loops over `compute_surface_analytics`, re-doing everything per name); `PreparedOptionProjection`/`PreparedHistoricalProjection` are properly prepared+parallel (`parallel_for_dynamic`) and deterministic. `dispersion_signal` is cheap by design (forward+IV only); `build_dispersion_book` costs 4 American evals per leg minimum (documented C1.7 price+vega optimization is in place).
- **Recomputation across calls:** `var_swap_vol` and `risk_neutral_density` at the *same* tenor from *different* call sites rebuild the grid (only the aggregate's copy-back dedupes); `skew_curvature` in `compute_surface_diff` re-resolves forward+ATM per surface per tenor. `fit_earnings_term` outer×inner search is ~41×(40+80) closed-form 2×2 solves — cheap, statically bounded.

## 4. WIRING — reachability gaps

- **`analytics.hpp` is not in the `vol.hpp` umbrella.** The flagship analytics module (plus `event_vol.hpp`, `earnings_term_fit.hpp`, `earnings_repro*.hpp`, `earnings_forecast_loader.hpp`, `vol_time.hpp`, `sr_tenor_grid.hpp`, `tearsheet.hpp`, `run_report.hpp`, `dense_slice.hpp`, `dispersion_strangle.hpp`, `strategy.hpp`) is absent from the one-include public API (`include/atx/vol/vol.hpp:82-183`), which does include projection/derivatives/dispersion.
- **`compute_surface_analytics` / RND / var-swap / implied-corr / CSV writers are reachable only from tests and `bench/analytics_bench.cpp`** — no example, CLI, run_report integration, or Python binding calls them (grep-verified).
- **Python bindings:** `bindings/analytics.cpp` exposes only the backtest `TearSheet` + TSV writers; `bindings/dispersion.cpp` exposes universe/backtest/schedule types but **not** `dispersion_signal`/`implied_correlation_*`; no binding for surface analytics, RND, eMove, event schedules, or projections beyond the backtest path.
- **`derivatives.hpp` is stranded on the legacy surface types**: templated on `EssviSurface`/`SviSurface` (surface.hpp), not `VolSurface`/`CurveSurface`/`PricedSurface` — the modern fitted pipeline cannot call `var_swap_fair_strike`/`deriv_price` without hand-converting slices; used only by its test (docs/reviews/pg-review already flagged wiring).
- `fit_earnings_term` not wired into `VolaSession`'s eMove (see P1-3). `phase_profile.cpp` does not exist (header-only; the scope list is stale). VolTime clock not threaded into fit/serve `TimeSpec` consumers beyond the repro tenor grid.

## 5. Verdict

The layer's strengths are real: a closed-form-validated density/BKM/MFIV stack, an SR-parity earnings-censored ATM pipeline with an unusually honest validation trail (the convention-sweep doc), a correct-by-construction hybrid vol-time clock, and disciplined engineering throughout (pure functions, Result-based errors, deterministic parallel batch paths, statically bounded loops). The top three risks: (1) **unit/convention fragmentation** — the 100× dispersion vega mismatch, three delta conventions, Julian-365.25 "Calendar365", and the VolTime/Calendar365 mixing in the earnings default are exactly the class of silent cross-module bias that erodes trust in every downstream number; (2) **event-vol identifiability in production** — the naive two-pillar eMove feeds all serving/analytics while the robust joint fit sits unwired, and the whole event model is ATM-lump-only with no off-ATM/W-smile decomposition; (3) **reachability** — the analytics flagship is invisible from the umbrella header and Python, and the vol-derivatives module is stranded on legacy surface types, so much of this section's value is currently un-shippable. Top three investments: (a) a convention lockdown pass (one vega unit, one delta convention with a vendor-compat mode, VolTime threaded end-to-end per SOTA B3, coordinated 365.25 decision); (b) promote `fit_earnings_term` (+ per-event weights, non-spanning σ_C, monotone-θ_C constraint) to the production eMove and extend the event model off-ATM; (c) productize the analytics: umbrella/Python exposure, a daily `SurfaceAnalytics` time-series store with cross-sectional ranks, and a realized-vol (Parkinson/GK/YZ) + carry suite — the three cheapest closures of the largest remaining gaps to a Vola-class offering.
